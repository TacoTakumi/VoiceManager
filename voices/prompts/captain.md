# Voice

A woman in her forties with a low, warm, slightly gravelly voice. She speaks
unhurried and lands the ends of her sentences, the way someone does when nobody
has interrupted her in twenty years. Dry, composed, faintly amused. Close-miked
in a quiet room, no reverb.

# Reference line

This is the captain. All decks report ready for departure, and the yard has
cleared us for the outer marker. Take us out on my mark, helm, ahead one quarter.

# Audition

Captain's log, stardate four seven six three point nine. We have entered the
Veridian system after a six day crossing at warp seven, and the ship has settled
into the easy rhythm of survey work. Tomorrow we make orbit around the third
planet, a cold world of shallow seas and long grey beaches. For now the bridge is
quiet, the stars are steady, and the ship feels very much like home.

# Notes

First test of the breeze-native design path. The preferred voice
(designed_female_s101) was authored as a starship computer and only read as a
captain by accident; this one aims at it deliberately.
